Frequently asked questions

Everything savers ask us about ISAs, allowances, transfers and our accounts.

General ISA questions

What is the ISA allowance for this tax year?
You can save up to £20,000 across all your adult ISAs in the 2026/27 tax year. Junior ISAs have a separate £9,000 allowance. See our ISA Guide for the full picture.
Do I pay tax on ISA interest?
No. Interest earned within an ISA is entirely tax-free and doesn't need to be declared to HMRC, regardless of your Personal Savings Allowance.
Can I have more than one ISA?
Yes, you can hold ISAs with multiple providers and split your allowance across different ISA types in the same tax year — but you can typically only pay new money into one Cash ISA within a tax year, unless it's a "flexible" ISA.
What happens if I go over my allowance?
HMRC monitors ISA subscriptions across providers. If you accidentally exceed your allowance, HMRC will usually contact you directly to correct it — get in touch with us straight away if you think this has happened.

Our accounts

What's the difference between Easy Access and Fixed Rate ISAs?
An Easy Access ISA lets you withdraw and deposit whenever you like, with a variable rate. A Fixed Rate ISA locks your rate and your money for a set term (1–3 years), usually paying a higher rate in return.
Can I open a Junior ISA for my grandchild?
Only a parent or legal guardian can open a Junior ISA and act as the registered contact, but grandparents and anyone else can contribute to it once it's open.
What happens when my Fixed Rate ISA matures?
Your balance automatically moves into our current Easy Access ISA unless you tell us otherwise before the maturity date, so your money stays tax-free without any gaps.
Is my money protected?
Yes. Eligible deposits with Earl Shilton Building Society are protected up to £85,000 per saver by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).

Transfers

Can I transfer my existing ISA to Earl Shilton?
Yes — see our ISA transfer page for the full step-by-step process. Never withdraw the funds yourself; always use the official transfer process to keep your tax-free status.
How long does a transfer take?
Cash ISA transfers typically complete within 15 working days from when your current provider receives the request.
