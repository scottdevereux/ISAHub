Common questions

ISA questions, answered

What is the ISA allowance for this tax year?
You can save up to £20,000 across all your adult ISAs in the 2026/27 tax year. You can split this across Cash ISAs, Stocks & Shares ISAs and other ISA types, but the combined total can't exceed your allowance.
Can I transfer my existing ISA to Earl Shilton?
Yes. We accept transfers from other providers' Cash ISAs without affecting your tax-free status, as long as it's done as a formal ISA transfer rather than a withdrawal. See our ISA transfer page for the full process.
Do I pay tax on ISA interest?
No. Interest earned within an ISA is entirely tax-free, with no need to declare it to HMRC, regardless of your Personal Savings Allowance.
