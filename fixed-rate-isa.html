How Fixed Rate ISAs work

You choose a term — 1, 2 or 3 years — and deposit a lump sum (plus, on some issues, further additions during a limited window after opening). Your rate is locked for the whole term, so market rate changes don't affect what you earn.

Because the rate is fixed, these accounts don't allow withdrawals or further deposits once the term begins. At maturity, your ISA automatically renews into our current Easy Access ISA unless you tell us otherwise, keeping your funds tax-free throughout.

Key product information

Good for

Savers who won't need the money during the term and want to guarantee today's rate against future changes.

Consider instead

If you might need access to your funds, our Easy Access ISA has no restrictions.
