Frequently asked questions

Everything business owners ask us about eligibility, our accounts, tax and protection.

Eligibility & getting started

Which businesses can open an account with you?
Sole traders, freelancers, limited companies, partnerships, charities and non-profits, clubs and societies, trusts and pension schemes, credit unions and cooperatives, and businesses holding client money. See our Business Types guide for eligibility and documents for each.
What documents will I need?
This depends on your business type, but generally includes proof of ID for signatories, proof of address, and evidence your business exists (a registration number or governing document). See the full checklist in our Application Guide.
Can I apply if my business was only recently formed?
Yes — newly registered companies, partnerships and charities can apply as soon as they're formed, provided you can supply the relevant registration documents and ID for signatories.
How long does an application take?
Straightforward applications, such as sole traders or single-director companies, can often be completed the same day. Businesses with several signatories, like charities or partnerships, may take a little longer while we verify all parties.

Our accounts

What's the difference between your five accounts?
The Business Bonus Saver has no notice period and pays 4.16% gross p.a./AER, including a bonus in years with no withdrawals. Our 90, 35 and 7 Day Notice accounts pay a fixed variable rate in exchange for that much notice before a withdrawal. The No Notice account offers instant access with no conditions at all, at a lower rate.
Can I open more than one business account?
Yes, you can hold multiple accounts with us at the same time — for example, a Business Bonus Saver for working capital alongside a 90 Day Notice account for longer-term reserves.
Why do the 7 Day and No Notice accounts pay less than the notice accounts?
The 7 Day Notice and No Notice accounts (formerly our Treasurers and Endeavour Accounts) predate our current range and carry their own rates. Our 35 and 90 Day Notice accounts, and the Business Bonus Saver, currently pay more in exchange for a longer notice period or a no-withdrawal condition.
Is there a maximum balance I can hold?
Yes — the maximum balance across all five accounts is £500,000 per business. Get in touch if your business needs to hold more than this.

Tax & protection

Is business savings interest tax-free, like an ISA?
No — business accounts don't carry a tax-free wrapper. Interest is paid gross (without tax deducted), and your business accounts for it through Corporation Tax, Self Assessment, or charitable exemptions as applicable. See our tax guide.
Is my business's money protected?
Eligible deposits are protected up to £120,000 per eligible depositor by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). Some larger companies and certain client money arrangements are treated differently — see our FSCS protection guide.
Is my company too large to be protected by the FSCS?
"Large companies" — broadly, those exceeding two of: turnover over £10.2m, balance sheet total over £5.1m, or more than 50 employees — are not automatically eligible depositors under FSCS rules. Most small and medium businesses are unaffected by this.
How does FSCS protection work for client money accounts?
It can work differently to a standard business account — FSCS may look through to protect each underlying client separately, rather than treating the whole account as one deposit, subject to conditions. See our Client Money Accounts section for more.
