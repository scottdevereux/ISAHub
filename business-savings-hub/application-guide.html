Applying for a business savings account

From choosing an account to your first deposit — here's exactly what happens, and what to have ready, whichever type of business you run.

How it works

Five steps from application to earning interest

  1. Choose your account

    Compare our Business Bonus Saver, 90/35/7 Day Notice and No Notice accounts, or use our choosing the right account guide if you're not sure.

  2. Confirm your business type

    Check the eligibility and document checklist for your structure in our Business Types guide — sole trader, company, partnership, charity, club, trust, credit union or client money account.

  3. Gather your documents

    Collect proof of ID for signatories, proof of address, and evidence your business exists — see the checklist below for a summary by business type.

  4. Complete your application and mandate

    Apply by phone, in branch, or start online. We'll verify your documents and set up your account mandate — who can operate the account, and how.

  5. Fund your account

    Once your account is open, deposit by bank transfer, cheque, or in branch, and your funds start earning interest as soon as they clear.

Setting up your mandate

Every business account needs a mandate — the instruction that tells us who can operate the account and how. When you apply, we'll ask you to confirm:

  • Who your authorised signatories are, by name
  • Whether instructions need one signature or several — for example, "any director" or "any two of three trustees"
  • Who to contact if signatories need to be added or changed in future

For companies this is usually set by board resolution; for partnerships, clubs, charities and trusts it's typically agreed and minuted by the partners, committee or trustees. Read more in our signatories and mandates guide.

How long does it take?

Most straightforward applications — sole traders and single-director companies — can be completed the same day. Charities, trusts and partnerships with several signatories may take a little longer while we verify all parties.

Before you start

Application FAQs

Can I apply if my business was only recently formed?
Yes — newly formed companies, partnerships and charities can apply as soon as they're registered, provided you can supply the relevant registration documents and ID for signatories.
Can I open more than one business account?
Yes, businesses can hold multiple accounts with us — for example, a Business Bonus Saver for working capital alongside a 90 Day Notice account for longer-term reserves.
What if my business doesn't fit neatly into one of the eight types?
Get in touch and describe your structure — many organisations are a variation on the types we've listed, and our team can confirm what documents we'd need.
