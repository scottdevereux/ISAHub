Business Easy Access Account

A variable rate savings account you can pay into and withdraw from whenever your business needs to — with no notice periods and no penalties.

Open this account Model your growth

How it works

The Business Easy Access Account is built for reserves that need to stay within reach — a VAT provision, a rainy-day fund, or surplus cash between projects. Deposit lump sums or regular transfers, and withdraw whenever your business needs to, without notice or penalty.

  • Unlimited free withdrawals and deposits
  • Interest calculated daily, paid annually or monthly
  • Manage your account online, by phone, or in branch
  • Open with as little as £1,000
  • Available to all business types — see our Business Types guide

Key product information

Interest rate3.85% AER / 3.85% gross (variable)
Minimum opening deposit£1,000
Maximum balance£1,000,000 per business
AccessUnlimited withdrawals, no notice or penalty
Interest paymentAnnually on the account anniversary, or monthly on request
Additional depositsAllowed at any time by bank transfer or in branch
Tax treatmentInterest paid gross — your business accounts for any tax due
Who can applySole traders, companies, partnerships, charities, clubs, trusts, credit unions and client money account holders

Rate shown is illustrative and correct as of publication only. Variable rates can go up or down. AER = Annual Equivalent Rate; Gross is the contractual rate before tax is deducted.

Is this account right for your business?

Good for

Operating reserves, tax provisions, or any cash your business might need at short notice.

Consider instead

If you can plan around 95 days' notice, or won't need the money for 1–2 years, our 95-Day Notice Account or Fixed Rate Bond usually pay a higher rate.

Getting started

How to open your Business Easy Access Account

  1. Check what you'll need

    Confirm the documents required for your business type in our Application Guide.

  2. Apply in minutes

    Get in touch by phone, in branch, or start online — we'll verify your business and set up your mandate.

  3. Fund your account

    Deposit from £1,000 upward by bank transfer, cheque, or in branch.

  4. Start earning interest

    Interest accrues daily from the moment your funds clear.
