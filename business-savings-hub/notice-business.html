Business 95-Day Notice Account

A better rate for the reserves you can plan around — give us 95 days' notice and withdraw penalty-free.

Open this account Model your growth

How it works

The 95-Day Notice Account suits businesses that can forecast their cash needs a few months ahead — a planned dividend, a tax bill, or reserves you're unlikely to touch on short notice. In exchange for giving us 95 days' notice before a withdrawal, you earn a higher variable rate than our Easy Access Account.

You can give notice at any time and make as many withdrawals as you like — there's just a 95-day wait once notice is given, and no penalty for doing so.

  • Higher variable rate than our Easy Access Account
  • Give notice any time — no limit on the number of withdrawals
  • Interest calculated daily, paid annually or monthly
  • Open with as little as £5,000
  • Available to all business types — see our Business Types guide

Good to know

Frequently asked about the 95-Day Notice Account

Can I give notice on part of my balance?
Yes. You can give notice on the full balance or just the amount you need — the rest keeps earning interest as normal.
What happens if I need funds before the 95 days are up?
You can request early access, but this may be subject to a loss of interest equivalent to 95 days on the amount withdrawn early. Speak to our team before doing so.
Can I cancel notice once I've given it?
Yes, you can cancel a notice request at any time before the withdrawal is made, and your funds will simply remain in the account.
