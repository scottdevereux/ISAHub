How the Fixed Rate Bond works

You choose a term — 1 or 2 years — and deposit a lump sum (plus, within a limited window after opening, further additions). Your rate is locked for the whole term, so market rate changes don't affect what your business earns.

Because the rate is fixed, this account doesn't allow withdrawals or further deposits once the term begins. At maturity, your balance automatically moves into our current Business Easy Access Account unless you tell us otherwise, so your reserves keep earning interest without any gaps.

Good for

Businesses that won't need the money during the term and want to guarantee today's rate against future changes.

Consider instead

If you might need access to your funds, our Easy Access Account or 95-Day Notice Account offer more flexibility.
