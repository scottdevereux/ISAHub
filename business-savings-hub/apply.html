5. Controlling individuals

This section must be completed in all cases. The first-named person will act as the representative shareholder or officer, authorised to attend meetings and exercise membership rights on behalf of the organisation's controlling individuals.

Under Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Common Reporting Standard (CRS) and Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) requirements, we must identify individuals who own or control more than 25% of the organisation's share capital, profits or voting rights, or otherwise exercise significant control, and obtain relevant tax residency information. If no such individual exists, provide the details of the senior managing official(s), such as a director, trustee or managing partner. For organisations limited by guarantee, provide the details of the senior member(s) who exercise control.

7. Deposit protection & classification

Earl Shilton Building Society is covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). The FSCS can pay compensation to depositors if a building society is unable to meet its financial obligations. Most depositors, including most individuals and businesses, are covered by the scheme — see our FSCS guide for a list of exclusions.

Please contact our business team directly before completing this application, as excluded entities need additional handling that we can't process through this online form.

We're required to record this classification when opening the account, and periodically thereafter.
