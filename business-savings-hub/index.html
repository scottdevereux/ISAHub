Common questions

Business savings questions, answered

Which businesses can open an account with you?
Sole traders, limited companies, partnerships, charities, clubs and societies, trusts and pension schemes, credit unions and cooperatives, and businesses holding client money can all apply. See our Business Types guide for eligibility and documents for each.
Is business savings interest tax-free, like an ISA?
No — business savings accounts don't carry a tax-free wrapper. Interest is paid gross (without tax deducted), and your business accounts for it through Corporation Tax, Self Assessment, or your charity's tax exemptions as applicable.
Is my business's money protected?
Eligible deposits are protected up to £120,000 per eligible depositor by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). Some larger companies and certain client money arrangements are treated differently — see our FSCS protection guide.
