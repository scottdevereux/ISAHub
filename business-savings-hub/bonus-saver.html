Business Bonus Saver

A no-notice savings account paying a strong rate that includes an annual bonus — as long as you don't withdraw during the interest year.

Open this account Model your growth

How it works

The Business Bonus Saver is built for reserves you don't expect to touch for a full interest year. There's no notice period on withdrawals, and the account pays 4.16% gross p.a./AER — a rate that includes a 1.50% bonus, paid only in interest years where no withdrawals are made.

You can still withdraw at any time if you need to. If you do, you simply earn the underlying rate — illustratively 2.66% gross/AER — for that interest year instead of the bonus rate, rather than losing access altogether.

  • 4.16% gross p.a./AER, including a 1.50% no-withdrawal bonus
  • No notice period — withdraw at any time if you need to
  • Interest calculated daily, paid annually or monthly
  • Open with as little as £1
  • Available to all business types — see our Business Types guide

Rate shown is illustrative and correct as of publication only. Variable rates can go up or down. Gross p.a. is the contractual rate before tax; AER shows what the rate would be if interest were paid and compounded once each year.

Is this account right for your business?

Good for

Businesses confident they won't need to dip into these reserves during the year, and want the strongest no-notice rate we offer.

Consider instead

If you expect to make withdrawals during the year, a 35 Day or 90 Day Notice account isn't bonus-dependent, so a withdrawal won't reduce your rate.
