7 Day Notice Account

Formerly known as our Treasurers Account — a short notice period that suits committees, treasurers and businesses that like to plan just a week ahead.

Open this account Model your growth

How it works

The 7 Day Notice Account — long known to many of our members as the Treasurers Account — has kept its name change simple: same account, same rules, now grouped alongside our other notice accounts. It suits committees, clubs and societies who like a small buffer before withdrawing, in exchange for a better rate than instant access.

You can give notice at any time and make as many withdrawals as you like — there's just a 7-day wait once notice is given, and no penalty for doing so.

  • 2.25% gross p.a./AER
  • Give notice any time — no limit on the number of withdrawals
  • Interest calculated daily, paid annually or monthly
  • Minimum opening deposit of £10,000
  • Available to all business types — see our Business Types guide

Good to know

Frequently asked about the 7 Day Notice Account

Is this the same account that used to be called the Treasurers Account?
Yes — we've renamed it to make our full account range easier to compare, but the account itself, its terms and its rate structure are unchanged for existing holders.
Can I give notice on part of my balance?
Yes. You can give notice on the full balance or just the amount you need — the rest keeps earning interest as normal.
What happens if I need funds before the 7 days are up?
You can request early access, but this may be subject to a loss of interest equivalent to 7 days on the amount withdrawn early. Speak to our team before doing so.
