Transfer your ISA to us

Move an existing Cash ISA — or a Junior ISA — to Earl Shilton without losing a penny of its tax-free status. We handle the process for you.

Start a transfer Understand ISA transfer rules
Important: never withdraw funds from an existing ISA to move them yourself — this loses the tax-free wrapper permanently. Always use the official ISA transfer process below.

How it works

The ISA transfer process, in four steps

  1. Choose your new ISA

    Pick the Earl Shilton ISA you'd like to transfer into — Easy Access, a Fixed Rate term, or a Junior ISA.

  2. Complete an ISA transfer form

    We'll ask for details of your existing ISA provider and account — no need to contact them yourself.

  3. We contact your current provider

    We request the transfer directly with your old provider, who sends the funds straight to us.

  4. Your funds land, tax-free status intact

    Cash ISA transfers typically complete within 15 working days. Your money keeps its tax-free wrapper throughout.

What you can transfer

Cash ISAs

Transfer in full or in part from any UK Cash ISA provider into an Easy Access or Fixed Rate ISA with us.

Junior ISAs & Child Trust Funds

Move a child's existing Junior ISA or eligible Child Trust Fund into our Junior Cash ISA.

We currently accept transfers of Cash ISA balances only. If your existing ISA includes Stocks & Shares holdings, these would need to be sold by your current provider first, or transferred as cash after encashment — speak to us for guidance.

Transferring a Fixed Rate ISA early?

Your current provider may apply an early exit charge or loss of interest — check their terms before starting a transfer.

Partial transfers

You can usually transfer just part of an ISA balance from previous tax years, while leaving the rest in place.

Before you start

Transfer FAQs

Does transferring my ISA use up my annual allowance?
No. Transferring an existing ISA doesn't count against your £20,000 annual allowance — that only applies to new money paid in during the current tax year.
How long does an ISA transfer take?
By industry standard, Cash ISA transfers should complete within 15 working days of your current provider receiving the transfer request.
Will I lose interest during the transfer?
Most providers continue paying interest on your balance until the funds actually leave their account, so there's typically no gap in interest.
