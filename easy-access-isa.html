Easy Access ISA

A tax-free variable rate ISA you can pay into and withdraw from whenever you need to — with no notice periods and no penalties.

Open this ISA Model your growth

How it works

The Easy Access ISA is built for savers who want their money working tax-free, without locking it away. Add lump sums or set up a standing order, and withdraw whenever life needs it — your tax-free ISA status is protected either way.

  • Unlimited free withdrawals and deposits
  • Interest calculated daily, paid annually or monthly
  • Manage your account online, by phone, or in branch
  • Open with as little as £1
  • Transfer in ISAs from other providers at any time

Key product information

Rate shown is illustrative and correct as of publication only. Variable rates can go up or down. AER = Annual Equivalent Rate, illustrates what the rate would be if interest were paid and compounded once each year.

Is this ISA right for you?

Good for

Building an emergency fund, saving toward a near-term goal, or simply wanting instant access to tax-free savings.

Consider instead

If you won't need the money for 1–3 years, a Fixed Rate ISA usually pays a higher rate.

Getting started

How to open your Easy Access ISA

  1. Check your allowance

    Confirm how much of your £20,000 annual ISA allowance you have left this tax year.

  2. Apply in minutes

    Get in touch by phone, in branch, or start online — we'll confirm your identity and ISA declaration.

  3. Fund your account

    Deposit from £1 upward by bank transfer, cheque, or in branch.

  4. Start earning tax-free interest

    Interest accrues daily from the moment your funds clear.
